Our development methodology

We adhere to a set of specifications and requirements in our projects that must be met to ensure proper functionality, integrity, security, and — above all — a stellar player experience.

1. Overview of Key Aspects

Hardware and software requirements: the Minimum Specifications for the Game to run. Standards governing the design and functionality of the game, including Error-Free code, Support for Reporting Errors, Integrity Checks and Recovery, and Data Encryption.

Platform Compatibility and Optimization: Ensuring the Game runs smoothly and consistently across operating platforms and devices, which will involve testing on different platforms and devices to identify and address compatibility issues, optimization of performance and graphics, and ensuring consistent game-play.

User Experience: Focuses on intuitive design, user-friendly interfaces, interesting story-lines and engaging game-play mechanics.

Data Handling and Privacy: Adhering to Data Protection Laws and Regulations, which includes obtaining user consent, Transparency in Agreements and Communication, and Age Restrictions

2. Importance of our Game Technical Standards

Ensuring Fair and Secure Gaming—this is to prevent fraud, ensure fair play, and to protect Players' Financial and Personal Information.

Facilitating Market Access—Making sure we're in Compliance with platform and regional regulations. This is essential for game release and success in various markets.

Enhancing User Trust and Satisfaction—Games that meet with High Technical Standards are perceived as more reliable, secure and enjoyable, which leads to greater player loyalty and positive brand reputation.

Driving Industry Growth—Standardization is believed to promote collaboration and innovation, leading to improvements in game quality, production efficiency, and overall industry advancement.

Protection Against Legal and Regulatory Penalties—We don't need legal issues, fines and platform removals

3. Best Practices for meeting Technical Standards

Plan Ahead—Define Clear Objectives, design documents, and quality assurance plans from the outset of game creation.

Test Frequently—Conduct regular and thorough testing throughout the development process.

Document Everything—Maintain detailed documentation of design decisions, code, assets, testing results and more.

Optimize the Game—Focus on Performance, Efficiency, and User Experience.

Incorporate Player Feedback—Gather and Implement Player Feedback in order to improve the Game.

Ensure Cross-Platform Compatibility—Test and Optimize for various Platforms and Devices.

Stay Updated on Regulations—keep on top of evolving regulations and guidelines related to game development and publishing.
