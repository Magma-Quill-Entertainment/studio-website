Setting a new standard for the video game industry

We love games — but the current state of the gaming industry leaves room for improvement. We started MagmaQuill to make games with reliable fundamentals and respect for players. Our projects are still in early development, but our standards are availible here.

Who We Are

We are a small collective of writers, designers, and developers from the United States and Canada who looked at the state of the gaming industry practically and decided to try our hand at running our own studio.

Do we have any idea what we are doing? No, but that's the exciting part: we haven't been told "No, that cannot be done." We are bullish, optimistic dreamers that plan to make a positive impact on the state of gaming.

We Believe In Great Ideas

Great games do not happen by accident. They come from teams that commit to readability, performance, accessibility, and long-term respect for their audiences.

We adhere to a set of specifications and requirements in our projects that must be met to ensure proper functionality, integrity, security, and — above all — a stellar player experience.

We call this commitment the MagmaQuill Gold Standard. The full framework lives on our About page.
